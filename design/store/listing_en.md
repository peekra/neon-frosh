## Full description

```text
Neon Frosh puts the time front and center: the hours in solid color, the minutes as an outline in a second color, framed by six data fields that show what matters to you. Light or dark background, eight colors, and a minimal always-on view on AMOLED watches.

Features (depending on watch support):

- Big time: hours solid in color 1, minutes as outline digits in color 2. Follows the 12 or 24 hour setting of your watch.

- Six data fields: a panel at the top, four round fields and a panel at the bottom. You choose what each one shows.

- Data to choose from: date, weather (temperature and condition), energy level, heart rate, active calories, total calories, steps, weekly run distance, weekly bike distance, weekly swim distance, weekly climbing time, weekly training time and VO2 max. Any field can also be left empty.

- Progress rings: round fields with a goal show a ring around them. Active calories fill up to 1000 kcal; heart rate, steps and energy level show their own scale.

- Colors: pick any of eight colors (red, orange, yellow, green, teal, blue, purple, pink) for the hours and rings, and another for the minutes and panels.

- Light or dark background, switchable at any time.

- Battery level and phone connection are shown at the top. The battery icon warns you when the charge is low.

- Always-on view on AMOLED watches: only the time as thin white outline digits, nudged slightly every minute to protect the screen. Watches with a standard display keep the full face.

- Touch and hold: on watches with a touchscreen, touching and holding a field opens the matching view on the watch (for example heart rate or steps). Not every data type has a view; swim distance, climbing time and training time do nothing.

- Settings: in the phone app, or on the watch itself via the watch face menu.

Notes: The date follows the language of your watch. Weather needs a connection to your phone. Climbing time is only offered on watches that record climbing. Values that are not available are shown as "--". Health values are for information only and are not medical advice.
```

## Permissions / privacy

```text
Complications: the face reads values your watch already has, such as energy level, VO2 max and weekly distances, so it can show them. User profile: the face reads your maximum heart rate (for the heart rate ring) and your profile data for the calorie calculation. All data stays on the watch; the face sends nothing anywhere.
```
